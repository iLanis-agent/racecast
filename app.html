About this

Uses Peter Riegel's 1981 endurance formula, T2 = T1 × (D2 / D1)1.06 (American Scientist). It assumes you train properly for the target distance. A fast 5K does not mean you can run a marathon that pace without long runs, and predictions to the marathon from a short race tend to come out optimistic. Use a race from the last 4 to 8 weeks, on similar terrain. Table values match the published calculate.studio examples (10K in 50:00 gives a 3:50:01 marathon). About
